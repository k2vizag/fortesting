# Video edit prompt (reusable – K2 Vizag style)

Copy everything inside the box, fill in the [brackets], and send it with your
new video link. Drive links must be set to "Anyone with the link".

```
Edit my new raw video into YouTube Shorts in my K2 Vizag style.
Use the same style as my earlier insurance videos (edit scripts are saved in
the repo k2vizag/fortesting under insurance/v2 – reuse them).

INPUT
- Raw video: [Google Drive link]
- Topic: [e.g. "Bike & car insurance" / "RC transfer" / "Aadhaar update"]
- Number of videos: [1 or 2]
  If 2: Video 1 = [part / topic 1 only], Video 2 = [part / topic 2 only].
  Tips or info common to both go in both videos.
- Title (top bars, whole video):
  Video 1: red bar "[TITLE LINE 1]" + yellow bar "[TITLE LINE 2]"
  Video 2: red bar "[TITLE LINE 1]" + yellow bar "[TITLE LINE 2]"
- Extra photos / clips for cutaways (optional): [links]
- Contact number for the end screen: 7989615350

1. CUTTING – watch and listen carefully, word by word
- Remove ALL pauses inside and between sentences (leave ~0.1 s).
- Remove "mmm / aaa / ఆ / అ" fillers, false starts, stammers and
  half/unfinished words before the real word.
- When I repeat a word or sentence, keep only the best complete take.
- Remove sentences that repeat information already given.
- Remove noise-only parts and anything that is not useful to the viewer.
- After editing, transcribe the final audio and confirm no cut-off or
  repeated words remain at any cut. Tell me exactly what you removed.
- Final speed 1.1x (natural voice pitch).

2. AUDIO
- Remove ALL background noise: traffic, horns, vehicles, fan, hum, hiss
  (AI voice denoiser, e.g. DeepFilterNet). Voice clear, loudness -14 LUFS.
- Soft background music under the voice, ducking while I speak.
- SFX must be SOFT, like my reference: soft bloop + low thump when a
  full-screen graphic comes in, soft bubble pop for stickers, soft tap for
  text/steps, soft wooden pluck for badges/prices.
  NO whoosh, NO ting / ding / metallic chimes. SFX always below my voice.

3. FRAMING
- Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920. Face centred but NOT heavily zoomed – show
  head, shoulders and some background. Blurred copy of the shot fills the
  top/bottom bands if needed.
- Gentle punch-in zoom (~7%) on alternate cuts to hide jump cuts.

4. CAPTIONS & STICKERS
- Telugu captions in my exact words (fix spelling), 2–3 words at a time,
  synced to speech.
- Place captions on the chest area, NEVER on my face.
- Style: white words + key word big in a yellow-orange gradient; small
  italic line under it when useful (e.g. meaning of a short form).
- App-icon style stickers with labels for key terms, and big numbers
  1 / 2 / 3 for tips or points – below/beside the face, never on it.

5. B-ROLL / GRAPHIC CARDS (like the reference video)
- Make full-screen graphic cutaways for the important parts: prices,
  amounts, comparisons, steps, documents, checklists, tips.
  Card types: white info card with coloured badge, dark timeline with
  steps that light up one by one, chart, photo card, typed checklist,
  "journey" card for year-wise or step-wise tips.
- Each card appears exactly when I say that point; my voice continues.
- Keep the title bars visible on top of the cards.
- Use my photos/clips if given; otherwise use clean drawn icons/graphics.

6. ENDING (last ~4 s)
- Dark end screen: YouTube icon, "లైక్ & సబ్‌స్క్రైబ్ చేయండి",
  LIKE / SUBSCRIBE buttons with click animation.
- Swinging board "కాల్ చేయండి 7989615350" at the top of the end screen
  (not at the bottom – YouTube text covers it).
- Do NOT show the contact number anywhere else in the video.

7. DELIVERY
- Send me the final MP4(s) and a short list of: what you cut, video length,
  and any Telugu words you were unsure about.
- Commit and push the edit scripts to my repo branch.
```

## Tips
- Record with the phone a little farther away and in a quiet place – less
  zoom-out and denoise needed.
- Stock photo sites are blocked in the editing environment, so send any real
  photos/clips (vehicle, documents, shop) as Drive links if you want them as
  B-roll.
- Say each point once, clearly; if you make a mistake, pause 2 seconds and
  repeat the full sentence – that makes the best take easy to keep.
